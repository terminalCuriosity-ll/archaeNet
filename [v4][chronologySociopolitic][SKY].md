### Danger Categories

| ++Category++ | ++Meaning++ |
|---|---|
| **6 · dead** | Lethal upon entry. |
| **5 · hell** | Require something like a constant protective / mechanized / hazmat suit for surface habitation. |
| **4 · risk** | Require a constant respirator, or otherwise have semi-consistent life-threatening environmental phenomena. |
| **3 · safe** | Require minimal habitation structures, or have isolated mild to dangerous phenomena (e.g. occasional coastal tsunamis or local freezing, deadly cold). |
| **2 · yidian** | Perfect for supporting human life or stable constructed environments. |
| **1 · port** | Constructed environments, simulated atmospheres, and zeaports (what we call space stations). |
| **\#/1** | **Constructed rating: hazard / constructed. The first number rates the hazard of the body or environment itself, and the 1 after the slash marks the constructed environment people actually live in, such as a station-city, habitat, synthetic atmosphere or simulated ecology (e.g., Hǎi and Lèshū: 6/1 = a dead planet with a constructed habitat).** |
